FAQ

How long can a video be?

30, 45 or 60 seconds. The final length follows your voiceover, so the last word is never cut off.

What do I get in the download?

A 720×1280 H.264 MP4 with the narration mixed in and the captions burned into the picture.

Which languages work?

English only for now. The voiceover and caption timing are both tuned for it.

Do I keep the rights?

Yes. We make no claim on what you make. Free renders carry a small StoryPlay mark in the corner; Pro removes it.

How do I log in?

With Google, GitHub or an email address. Nothing else is required.

Will my videos disappear?

Free renders are deleted an hour after they finish; Pro keeps yours for seven days. Either way, download before the window closes.
