How it works

Four steps. The interesting part happens after you press create, on a server, while you wait.

Paste your story

Bring a Reddit post, a true story, or something you wrote yourself. Between 50 and 3000 characters. If you would rather not paste anything, the generator can write a draft for you from a one-line prompt, and you can edit it before rendering.

Pick a game and a style

Choose the gameplay, a voice, a caption style and colour, and roughly how long you want it. Thirty, forty-five and sixty seconds are the options.

The render runs on the server

Your job goes into a queue and is picked up by a background worker. It is not processed by the page you are looking at. The worker strips formatting from the script, generates the voiceover, measures how long that audio actually is, then works out caption timings and picks a gameplay segment in parallel. It reports progress at each stage, so you can watch it move.

Download it

Gameplay and narration are combined with the captions burned into the picture and written out as an H.264 MP4 at 720×1280. Finished videos are deleted from the server an hour after they are created, so download before you close the tab.

If a render fails

Failed jobs are retried twice with a short delay before they are marked as failed. If a retry also fails you get the error text on the job rather than a silent hang. Common causes are the text-to-speech service being unreachable or FFmpeg running out of disk space.
