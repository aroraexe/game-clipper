Why there are no paid plans

There is no billing system behind this yet, so there is nothing to upgrade to. If paid plans are added later they will appear here, and nothing you have made will change or be taken away.

The actual limits

Usage is limited by the server rather than by a plan. Renders are processed one or two at a time depending on the machine, and finished videos are deleted from the server an hour after they are created. Heavy use may queue or be refused; there is no quota to buy more of.
